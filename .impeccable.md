# Design Context

## Audience

Telegram-first buyers using branded commerce bots to purchase digital goods
with crypto. They are usually on a phone and expect the interaction to be as
fast and obvious as a native Telegram menu.

## Core jobs

- Find the kind of product they want without scanning a wall of buttons.
- Narrow to a specific product or duration with as few taps as practical.
- Understand sold-out states, price ranges, and navigation at a glance.

## Brand personality

Compact, direct, commerce-first, and quietly polished. The bot should inherit
the tenant's branding while the interaction model feels like uSwap: structured,
confident, and data-driven rather than improvised.

## Interface principles

- Use progressive disclosure for mixed or crowded catalog levels.
- Let `catalog.level` metadata own taxonomy, ordering, and layout decisions.
- Keep each Telegram screen short enough to scan without scrolling.
- Prefer plain, human labels over internal IDs or shouty all-caps taxonomy.
- Avoid dead-end intermediary screens and preserve clear Back/Home navigation.
